Co-creative storytelling formats

Explore different ways a story can feel

These five prototypes show possible storytelling formats for the Building Bridges platform. Open each experience, compare them, and use the survey to tell us which format feels most engaging, safe, and meaningful.

📷
Memory-based

Memory Album

A scrapbook-style experience using memories, reflections, and visual moments.

🃏
Card-based

Story Card Deck

A swipeable card journey that breaks the story into small emotional moments.

🌍
Journey-based

Interactive Globe Journey

A spatial story format where movement, place, and identity become part of the experience.

✨
Scroll-based

Immersive Scroll Story

A cinematic scrolling story with chapters, transitions, and emotional pacing.

🎙️
Audio-based

Kinetic Audio Story

A voice-inspired storytelling format using audio rhythm, animated words, and atmosphere.

Note: These are exploratory prototypes for feedback and co-creation. In the final platform, stories would only be created or published through a safe review process with privacy controls and project approval.
